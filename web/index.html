HOW IT WORKS

Everything runs in your browser. The camera image never leaves your device.

1

Pick a game

Choose Catching Fish, Fruit Ninja or Tetris and press Play.

2

Allow the camera

Your browser asks once. No camera? Mouse and keyboard work too.

3

Play with your hands

MediaPipe tracks 21 points on each hand in real time.

Catching Fish

The game is catching fish. An exciting adventure inspired by the beloved film "Finding Nemo". In order not to starve the shark, you have to eat the Nemo. Just open and close your hand for the shark to catch the Nemo. You have 60 seconds, be quick. Be careful, don't eat the blowfish while you try to catch the Nemo.

🏆 Engineering Project of the Year '23

Tricoders, a real-life game controlled with human hand gestures, received the Engineering Project of the Year award at Beykoz University in the 2022–2023 third-grade category.

We wanted to re-present familiar games with an innovative idea: instead of a keyboard, the games follow your hand movements, so you can experience them in a more physical way.
